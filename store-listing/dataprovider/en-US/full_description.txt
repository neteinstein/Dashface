Dashface data is the free companion for the Dashface watch face on Wear OS. It adds four complications to your watch:

• Next meeting: the next event from the calendar synced to your watch, "Now" while it runs, or "No more meetings today"
• Following meeting: the event after that
• Stress (heart-rate estimate): how far your heart rate is above your usual resting rate, from 0 to 100
• Skin temperature: your wrist skin temperature in °C or °F, on watches with a skin temperature sensor

They are made for Dashface, and also work as complications on other watch faces that accept long text, short text or ranged values.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Install Dashface data and the Dashface watch face on your watch.
2. Pick Dashface as your watch face. Its meeting rows, stress bar and thermometer use these complications.
3. Tap a row or bar that says "Tap" to allow access. You can also open Dashface data from the app list to see and grant everything in one place.

EASY ON THE BATTERY
• Meetings: the whole day is handed to the watch at once, and the watch switches rows when meetings start and end. The app never wakes on a timer for them.
• Stress: reuses heart-rate readings the watch already takes, and only while stress is on your watch face.
• Skin temperature: one quick reading every 15 minutes at most, only while the watch face is showing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet access and no analytics
• Your calendar, heart rate and temperature never leave the watch
• Each permission is explained before it is asked, and only when you tap

PERMISSIONS
• Calendar: to show your next meetings
• Heart rate (body sensors): for the stress estimate
• Skin temperature (body sensors): for the thermometer
• Health data in the background (optional): to keep the stress estimate and thermometer up to date while the screen is off
• Run at startup: to resume the stress estimate after a restart, only if you use it

REQUIREMENTS
Wear OS 5 or later. Skin temperature needs a watch with a skin temperature sensor; some watch makers keep the sensor private to their own apps.

The stress estimate and skin temperature are wellness hints, not medical measurements. Exercise raises the stress estimate too. Dashface data is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Dashface is open source: github.com/neteinstein/Dashface
